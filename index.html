How we work

A clear path from idea to launch.

Short cycles, working software at every step, and no surprises about scope, timeline or cost.

  1. Discover

    We learn your business, users and constraints, then agree on goals, scope and a realistic plan.

  2. Design

    User flows, interface designs and a technical architecture you can review before any code is written.

  3. Build

    Development in short iterations with regular demos, automated tests and builds you can try on your own devices.

  4. Launch & support

    Store submission, production rollout and monitoring — then ongoing maintenance, updates and new features.

About

Small studio. Serious engineering.

HashFoundry is an independent software company. We work closely with founders and businesses to turn ideas into dependable products — and we stay around after launch to keep them running well.

The name says how we work: software shaped with care, then tested until it holds.

  • Security by default

    Encryption, least-privilege access and careful handling of keys and personal data from the first commit.

  • Ship early, ship often

    You see working software within weeks, and every iteration ends with something you can use and judge.

  • Transparent and yours

    Clear estimates, open communication, and documented source code you can take forward with or without us.

App support

Using one of our apps and need help? Email hello@hashfoundry.com and include:

  1. The app name and version (shown in the app's Settings).
  2. Your device model and operating system version.
  3. What you expected to happen, what happened instead, and a screenshot if possible.

Your account and data

To ask what data an app holds about you, or to request its deletion, email us with the subject "Data request" and we'll guide you through it.

Never share your recovery phrase, passwords or private keys with anyone — HashFoundry staff will never ask for them.
